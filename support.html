Help & support

Contact

The quickest way to reach us is in the app: tap your initial at the top, then Send feedback. Pick Idea, Problem or Other and write what you need. Every message is read.

Common questions

How do I add a friend? Open the Friends tab and type their friend code, or send them your invite link from the profile menu. They accept, and then you can compare.

Who can see my rankings and notes? Only friends you've accepted. See the privacy policy for details.

How do I report or block someone? In the Friends tab, tap Report under their name. Blocking removes them and stops their friend requests. For a request you don't want, tap Block.

How do I delete my account? Tap your initial at the top, then Delete my account. This removes everything for good.

Where do movie details come from? From TMDB and OMDb. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
