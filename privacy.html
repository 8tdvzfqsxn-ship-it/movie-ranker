Privacy policy

Last updated November 27, 2026

Film Ranker is a small app for ranking movies and TV shows and comparing them with friends. This page explains what it stores, who can see it, and how to remove it. The short version: it keeps only what the app needs, shows your rankings only to friends you accept, and never sells or shares your information for advertising.

What Film Ranker stores

  • Your account: your email address and password (the password is stored scrambled, so no one can read it), and the first name you choose.
  • Your friend code: a short code made for you, so friends can add you.
  • What you rank: the movies and shows you rank, their scores, the moments you pick and any notes you write.
  • Friends: who you're friends with, friend requests, the Friend or Best friend label you give each one (only you see your labels), and anyone you block.
  • Feedback and reports you send from the app, with the app version and screen size, to help fix problems.

On your device, the app also remembers a few settings, such as which My list view you like. These stay on the device.

Who can see it

  • Friends you accept can see your name, your rankings, moments and notes, so you can compare.
  • No one else can see your rankings. People who aren't your friends see nothing.
  • Feedback and reports are read only by the app's keeper.

Services Film Ranker uses

  • Supabase stores accounts and data, and handles sign-in.
  • TMDB (The Movie Database) and OMDb supply movie and show details, posters and what's trending. Searches go to them through Film Ranker's own server, so they don't receive your name or email.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the app's pages.

Film Ranker has no ads and no tracking, and it does not sell or rent your information to anyone.

Deleting your information

You can delete your account at any time: open the app, tap your initial at the top, then Delete my account. This removes your account, rankings, moments, notes, friends, blocks and feedback for good. It can't be undone.

Children

Film Ranker is not meant for children under 13, and doesn't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

Questions
